## Tips for Best Results

- **Be specific** - Include details like cluster, action type (RIA/IA), duration, or number of partners
- **Ask follow-ups** - The AI remembers your conversation context
- **Use call IDs** - Reference specific calls by their ID (e.g., HORIZON-CL5-2024-D3-01)
- **Request more** - If you need more results, just ask "show me more" or "any others?"
